## Key Takeaway

The three pillars work synergistically — no single pillar provides complete protection. The governance-containment gap is the industry's blind spot: 58% have monitoring but only 37% have true containment. Defense requires **guardrails to prevent**, **permissions to gate**, and **auditability to prove**.
