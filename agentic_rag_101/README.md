# Agentic RAG 101

A self-paced course on Agentic Retrieval Augmented Generation — how combining RAG with autonomous AI agents creates systems that can dynamically retrieve, reason over, and integrate real-time data into LLM responses.

## Parts

1. [Understanding RAG and Agents](part1_understanding_rag_and_agents.md) — Foundations of RAG and AI agents, and why combining them matters.
2. [What is Agentic RAG?](part2_what_is_agentic_rag.md) — How Agentic RAG works, with a step-by-step customer support example.
3. [Agentic RAG Capabilities](part3_agentic_rag_capabilities.md) — The seven key features: dynamic retrieval, autonomous decisions, context awareness, scalability, reduced hallucination, customizable workflows, and multi-step reasoning.
4. [Types of Agentic RAG](part4_types_of_agentic_rag.md) — Single-agent, multi-agent, and hierarchical architectures.
5. [Implementing Agentic RAG](part5_implementing_agentic_rag.md) — Hands-on resources for building with LangChain, LlamaIndex, Pinecone, and LangGraph.
6. [Challenges and Future Directions](part6_challenges_and_future_directions.md) — Current limitations and where the field is headed.
