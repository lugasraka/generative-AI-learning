Which Agent Type?

A decision tool based on Part 2 of the Agentic AI Crash Course. Answer 4 questions; get a recommendation.

1. Is the problem repetitive and well-structured?
2. Does it involve natural language understanding or generation?
3. Is it a multi-step task with decisions along the way?
4. Does it need a human in the loop?
